At the edge of a quiet town, a small library opened its windows to the morning. Mira placed a blue notebook on the table and began a list of things she wanted to remember. The sound of rain on the roof. The smell of fresh bread. A story her grandfather told about a boat that never left the river.

She turned the page. Outside, the baker waved to a neighbor, and a bicycle bell rang once. Nothing hurried. Mira read a chapter, closed her eyes for a moment, and found that she could still picture every room in the story.

Later that afternoon, she walked to the river with the notebook in her pocket. The water moved slowly beneath the bridge. She sat on a bench and wrote one final sentence: a good story waits for you to return. Then she put down her pen and watched the clouds change shape.
